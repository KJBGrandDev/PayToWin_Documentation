Game Design

Concept, Genre & Target Players

Pay to Win is a single-player, third-person tactical horde survival shooter built in Unity. Inside the quarantined swamp Dead Zone, every infected kill earns blood money ($) toward seven hospital ICU bills—creating a constant moral tension between spending cash to survive the next wave and leaving enough behind when you inevitably fall.

Core Hook Spend to Survive vs. Save Family

Spending in the Salvage Armory ([B]) buys guns, 5-star upgrades, healing Field Stims, and Armor Plates—but visibly drops your live ICU Family Ladder rungs from SAVED back to SPENT. Dying with under $1,000 saves nobody, regardless of round reached.

Target Players Ages 16+

Fans of wave-based survival shooters (Left 4 Dead, Killing Floor, Call of Duty Zombies) who want high-impact gunplay paired with risk-reward resource tension and branching narrative endings.

Full Storyline, Cutscene & Character Profiles

After a reckless night behind the wheel leaves all seven members of his family on life support behind hospital ICU glass, the protagonist is handed a medical bill he can never pay alone. Outside the waiting room, a mysterious stranger offers an impossible bargain: enter the demonic swamp Dead Zone and harvest bounties against the unholy plague—trading his life and bullets for their tomorrow.

Each game launch opens the first PLAY transition with a 53-second voiced Story Intro Cutscene (PayToWin_Story_Intro.mp4), complete with an upper-right SKIP STORY >> button revealed after 3.0s for returning players. Subsequent replays in the same sitting deploy straight into Day 1 without repeating the intro.

Gameplay Mechanics & Rules

  • 7-Day Escalation Loop: Survive waves across Days 1–7 inside the active Combat Zone. Wave completion awards Salvage Cash and triggers a timed Buying Phase / Respite Sanctuary window.
  • Combat Zone Boundary: A 360° yellow caution-tape perimeter surrounds the fight area. Stepping outside starts a warning countdown before inflicting rapid damage—and resets cleanly whenever returning to Menu or restarting.
  • Dual-Mode Salvage Armory ([B]): Opens during Buying Phase with three tabs: Workbench & Ammo (weapon unlocks, 5-star gun part upgrades, and ammo packs), Field Stims (bought directly into Field Pack), and Armor Plates ($120 per plate). During active combat, [B] triggers Quick Ammo Resupply (brief slow-mo reprieve, 15s cooldown); pressing [F] refills all carried guns in one click.
  • Snappy Weapon & Aim Handling: Includes aligned weapon slots 1–6, camera-true heavy weapon aiming with close-range barrel sweep detection (preventing point-blank misses), and a fast 0.45s Vulcan Minigun spin-up.
  • Vitals, 5-Plate Armor Rig & Field Pack ([I]): Health, Stamina, and a 5-plate blue Armor Rig absorb hits. Healing syringes, regen serums, stamina surges, and adrenaline shots are stored in the Backpack ([I] / [TAB]) for emergency use.
  • Live 3D Title & Clean Pause Reset: Opens with a live 3D campfire diorama title screen and includes a centered WARNING · RESET PROGRESS? confirmation modal when abandoning a mid-run game to Main Menu.

Technical

Engine, Version & Tools Used

  • Game Engine: Unity 2022.3.62f3 LTS
  • Render Pipeline: Universal Render Pipeline (URP 14.0.12) with Built-in barrier shader compatibility restoration
  • Language & Runtime: C# (.NET Framework 4.7.1 / Mono)
  • UI, Video & Typography: Unity UGUI, TextMesh Pro (SairaCondensed), Unity VideoPlayer (PayToWin_Story_Intro.mp4), and runtime Resources.Load<Texture2D> Field Manual boards
  • Character Framework: Invector Third-Person Controller (Invector-3rdPersonController)
  • Tooling & Asset Pipelines: Visual Studio / VS Code, Git, Python / Pillow (annotated Field Manual guides) & imageio-ffmpeg (38s trailer mix pipeline)

How to Build & Run the Game

  1. Install Unity 2022.3.62f3 LTS via Unity Hub with the Windows Build Support (IL2CPP or Mono) module.
  2. Clone the repository and open the Unity project folder in Unity Hub.
  3. Open Assets/_Project/Scenes/World_SwampSurvival_Map.unity (or World_OpenSurvival_Map.unity) in the Project window.
  4. Press Play in the Unity Editor to test immediately (or open File → Build Settings, select PC, Mac & Linux Standalone → Windows (x86_64), verify the active scene is in Scenes In Build, and click Build And Run).

Known Bugs & Limitations

  • Windows PC Only: Input prompts, cursor locking, and shop navigation are tuned for Windows keyboard & mouse; macOS, Linux, and mobile builds are untested.
  • QA Unlock Toggle Default: In build v2.0.68, SurvivalDebugTester.unlockAllWeapons defaults to true for weapon showcase/testing. Press F9 (or set to false) to test normal progression unlocking.
  • Benign Compiler Warnings: Unity console reports 3 pre-existing unused-field warnings (CS0414 in ZombieHealth.cs, PlayerFlashlightController.cs, and TacticalArsenalStoreHUD.cs) with 0 compile errors; all new systems compile with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
  • Testbed Scene Scope: Zombie_Combat_Testbed.unity is intended for isolated combat tuning and does not represent the full 7-day open-survival progression flow.

Lessons Learned

  • Make Narrative Stakes Visible Every Second: Moving the 7 family hospital bills into the live top-left ICU Family Ladder turned abstract money numbers into an immediate emotional dilemma every time a player eyes an upgrade in the Shop.
  • Design Onboarding for Zero-Confusion Reading: Combining crisp in-game screenshots with short, large-print plain English (26pt) in a searchable full-screen manual communicates mechanics far faster than dense paragraphs.
  • Practicality Beats Over-Stylized UI: Early dark-yellow/olive shop palettes failed readability under between-wave timers; neutral slate surfaces paired with clear semantic colors (Emerald = saved/affordable, Amber = action, Crimson = spent/danger) solved legibility cleanly.
  • Verify Controls & Cursor State at Every Boundary: Centralizing blocking checks (AreAnyBlockingMenusOpen()) and resetting zone/horde state cleanly prevented stuck-input or background timer bugs across transitions.
